# Business Case

## Objective

Create a practical evaluation harness for deciding whether AI agents and RAG workflows are ready to pilot, scale, or stop.

## Problem

AI teams often ship agent demos without a consistent release-readiness framework. This creates risk when demos move into business workflows without evidence of task success, grounding, cost control, latency performance, safety, and approval compliance.

## Stakeholders

- AI engineering teams
- Product owners
- Enterprise architects
- Risk and compliance leaders
- AI transformation executives
- Model governance teams

## Value proposition

Agent Eval Harness gives teams a repeatable benchmark pattern that converts agent runs into business-readable scorecards. It helps leaders compare options and make release decisions with evidence.

## Expected impact

- More disciplined agent and RAG release reviews
- Faster identification of weak prompts, tools, or retrieval strategies
- Better visibility into cost-quality-latency tradeoffs
- Stronger governance alignment before scale-up
- Reusable benchmark packs across domains and teams
